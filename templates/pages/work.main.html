whatsapp-persona-bot

Personal tool · runs locally

A WhatsApp auto-responder that replies in my own voice while I’m offline. It connects as a linked device, learns my writing style from the messages I actually send, and uses the Claude API to answer incoming messages, including first responses to freelance leads.

The interesting part is the guard rails rather than the AI call. Replies are capped per chat per hour and sent after a randomised delay. The bot goes quiet in any chat the moment I reply myself, and by default it stays out of group chats and never answers numbers on an ignore list. It is instructed never to quote prices or commit to deadlines, and every message it sends is logged so I can review it. Node.js 20, Baileys, the Anthropic SDK and pino, kept running under pm2.

It’s a small tool I built for myself, about 450 lines with no test suite, so judge my engineering on RentManager. I’m including it because it’s how I handle AI in practice: useful, but kept on a short leash.
